Frequently Asked Questions

  • Navigate to the Live Tracking tab on the left sidebar. The interactive vector map will show the real-time position of your child's assigned bus, upcoming stops, ETA countdown, and vehicle speed governor status.

  • Open the My Children page and click the Add Child button. Fill in the student's full name, school, grade, and pickup stop. You can also edit or remove child profiles at any time.

  • You can submit a request using the Route or Stop Change category in the support form above, or contact your school's transport desk directly via the helpline phone number.

  • Visit the Payments section. In the billing history table, click the Receipt button next to any transaction to open a printable official tax invoice.

  • Go to the Route & Stops page to view your assigned driver's verified profile, mobile contact number, and bus attendant information, with a direct Call Driver trigger.
