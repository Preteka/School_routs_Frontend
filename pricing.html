COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SchoolRoute subscription plans and payments.

Every SchoolRoute plan includes live GPS bus tracking, instant arrival notifications, RFID student check-in alerts, and standard digital support. Higher plans include additional benefits such as priority support, multi-guardian alerts, dedicated concierge service, and guaranteed bus seat lock.

Billing is based on your selected plan term. The Monthly Plan is billed at ₹999 per month, the Quarterly Plan at ₹2,499 every 3 months, and the Yearly Plan at ₹8,999 for a complete 10-month school year. Payments are processed once at the start of each term.

Plan modifications and refund policies are governed by your school's transport guidelines and can be processed via the Parent Dashboard or by contacting our support team. Upgrade requests can be applied mid-term where applicable.

We support all major payment modes including UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Visa/Mastercard debit and credit cards, and Net Banking across 50+ major banks. All transactions are encrypted and processed securely.

Yes. Official GST-compliant tax invoices with student and school details are generated instantly after every successful payment. They are permanently stored in your Parent Dashboard for download at any time, including for tax claims or reimbursement purposes.

Yes. The Parent Dashboard provides complete subscription management — view current plan details, check payment history, download invoices, update billing information, and initiate plan changes or support requests, all from one centralized interface.

If you need to adjust your transportation plan mid-term — such as changing bus routes, stop locations, or plan tier — contact our support team or raise a request through the Parent Dashboard. Changes are processed in coordination with your school's transport office as per their scheduling policies.
