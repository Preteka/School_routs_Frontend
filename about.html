OUR STORY

Technology With a Purpose

SchoolRoute was created with a simple idea: parents should never have to wonder where their child is during the journey to or from school.

We bring real-time transportation visibility, verified personnel, route information, student boarding records, and digital communication together in one connected platform.

From tracking a bus on the map to receiving an arrival notification, SchoolRoute helps families stay informed while schools and transport teams manage operations with greater confidence.
